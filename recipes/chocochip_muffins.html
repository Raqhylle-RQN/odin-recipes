Chocolate Chip Muffins

Description

Chocolate Chip Muffins are sweet, bite-sized baked goods mixed with delicious chocolate chips. Their golden-brown tops, soft interiors, and rich chocolate flavor make them a satisfying treat for any time of the day. These muffins offer a simple yet enjoyable combination of sweetness and texture, and can be served as a breakfast treat, an afternoon snack, or a dessert to share with family and friends.

This recipe brings together everyday baking ingredients to create a batch of homemade chocolate chip muffins. The milk, vegetable oil, and egg provide moisture, while the flour, sugar, and baking powder help form the muffin's soft texture. Mini chocolate chips are added to give each muffin a sweet chocolatey taste. These muffins are best enjoyed warm and freshly out of the oven.

Prep Time 10 mins
Cook Time 20 mins
Total Time 30 mins
Servings 12

Ingredients:

  • ¾ cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup white sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup mini semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 ½ tablespoons white sugar
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C). Grease a 12-cup muffin tin or line cups with paper liners.
  2. Combine milk, oil, and egg in a small bowl until well blended.
  3. Combine flour, 1/2 cup sugar, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl, making a well in the center.
  4. Pour milk mixture into well and stir until batter is just combined; fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Spoon batter into the prepared muffin cups, filling each 2/3 full.
  6. Combine 1 ½ tablespoons white sugar and 1 tablespoon brown sugar in a small bowl; sprinkle on tops of muffins.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven until tops spring back when lightly pressed, about 18 to 20 minutes.
  8. Cool in the tin briefly, then transfer to a wire rack. Serve warm or cool completely.
